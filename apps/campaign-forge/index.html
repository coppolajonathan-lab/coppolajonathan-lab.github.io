Pitch an idea in a sentence or a paragraph. You'll get back a starter kit: the hook, the threat, three paths forward, NPCs, and encounter seeds, plus a few questions to sharpen it.

🔐 To enter the forge, open ⚙ Settings and solve the riddle for the passphrase.

Settings

📜 A riddle carved above the door

Three words spoken as one breath. No gaps between them, and each word stands tall at its start.

The first two are the battle-cry of any party that walks in together: two short words that declare who stands here.

The third is what the bards call those who charge when fear says flee. It rhymes with gallant, and a certain comic-strip prince wore it as his name.

Speak it as the party would, about themselves.
